## Prompt 3 (Best)

You are a professional travel copywriter.

Generate:
- Catchy title
- 80-120 word description

Input:
Mountain Escape
Manali
WiFi, Fireplace, Mountain View

Output:
Professional and attractive description.

## Best Prompt

Prompt 3 produced the most natural and engaging results. It generated a structured response with a title and a descriptive paragraph suitable for users viewing homestays.
